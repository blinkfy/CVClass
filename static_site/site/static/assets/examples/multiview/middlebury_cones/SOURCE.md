# Middlebury Cones Stereo Sample

- Source: Middlebury Stereo 2003 Cones quarter-size dataset.
- Page: https://vision.middlebury.edu/stereo/data/scenes2003/
- Permission note: https://vision.middlebury.edu/stereo/data/ states that the images and disparity maps may be used and published, with requested citation of the appropriate papers.
- Files used here: `im2.png` and `im6.png`.
- Use in this project: real two-view geometry demo for SIFT matching, fundamental matrix, essential matrix, pose recovery, triangulation, and reprojection error.
